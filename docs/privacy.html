Last updated: September 17, 2026

Overview

GreatClipsDeal.com ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website at greatclipsdeal.com (the "Site").

The short version: We collect minimal data, don't sell your information, and use standard analytics to improve our site. That's it.

Information We Collect

  • Email Address: If you sign up for coupon alerts, we collect your email address.
  • Contact Form: If you contact us, we collect your name, email, and message.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Analytics Data: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This includes pages visited, time on site, and general location (city/country level).
  • Cookies: We use essential cookies to make the site work properly and analytics cookies to understand usage patterns.
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, and screen size to ensure our site displays correctly.

How We Use Your Information

  • To send you coupon alerts (if you subscribed)
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To improve our website and user experience
  • To analyze site traffic and usage patterns
  • To prevent fraud and abuse

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We use third-party services like Google Analytics, Formspree (contact forms), and email services. These providers have their own privacy policies.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

WhatsApp coupon requests

We test different coupon popup designs using a random browser identifier stored locally for up to 90 days. We record the assigned variation, popup views, button clicks and dismissals, and connect requested-coupon outcomes to that variation to measure verified membership and delivery. These counts identify browsers, not people across devices. Preview links are excluded, and the experiment respects browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. Individual experiment records are removed after 90 days; anonymous aggregate test results may be retained.

If you request a coupon through WhatsApp, we store your selected offer, request code, consent record, WhatsApp number, display name, group membership result and delivery status. We use these details to match your request to Deal Dropper membership and automatically deliver that coupon privately. A keyed hash of your IP address helps limit abuse. Joining Deal Dropper means receiving its Amazon deal and coupon group posts; it does not enroll you in ongoing private marketing messages.

Your number and profile may be visible to other group members. WhatsApp also processes messages under its own privacy policy. Reply STOP to cancel a pending request, leave the group to stop group posts, or contact us to request deletion of our request records. We remove request records and local delivery receipts after 90 days through scheduled cleanup; this does not delete messages already held by WhatsApp or other members. Membership checks and delivery use our existing linked-device bot and require it to be online.
