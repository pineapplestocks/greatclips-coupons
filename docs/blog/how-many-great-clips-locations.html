How Many Great Clips Locations Are There?

4,303 salons, 2,550 cities, and five major cities with none at all

Published August 23, 2026 · By Kumar Chaudhari, GreatClipsDeal

📊 Key findings

  • There are 4,303 Great Clips salons across 2,550 US cities — in all 50 states and the District of Columbia.
  • Texas leads with 445, ahead of Florida (290), Ohio (284) and California (277).
  • 1,827 of those cities — 72% — have exactly one salon. This is a suburban chain, not a big-city one.
  • There is not a single Great Clips inside New York City, Philadelphia, Boston, Detroit or Miami.

Great Clips is one of the largest hair salon brands in the world, but public figures for it are usually rounded to "4,000+" or quoted for North America as a whole. Counting the official salon locator directly gives 4,303 salons in the United States, spread across 2,550 cities.

The cities with the most salons

The single biggest concentration is not where you would guess. San Antonio, TX has 54 Great Clips inside the city limits — more than any other city in the country, and 5 times as many as Chicago, which has 10.

Why the big cities are missing

Five of the largest cities in America — New York, Philadelphia, Boston, Detroit, Miami — have no Great Clips at all inside the city limits. That is not an error in the data. Great Clips builds in strip malls and suburban retail centres with parking, which is exactly the format dense urban cores do not have.

Those metros are still well covered, just not downtown. The salons nearest New York City are across the river in New Jersey, and the Detroit market runs through its suburbs — Ann Arbor, MI, Rochester Hills, MI, Troy, MI, Canton, MI among them.

How spread out the chain is

The clearest way to see the strategy is the distribution: 1,827 of 2,550 cities have exactly one salon, and the average across all cities is 1.7. Great Clips grows by adding towns, not by stacking salons in the same downtown. We group those cities into 646 metro markets, which is also how the company scopes most of its coupons.
