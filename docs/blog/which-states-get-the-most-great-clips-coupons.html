Which States Get the Most Great Clips Coupons?

Six months, 960 coupons, 44 states — and one very clear winner

Published July 6, 2026 · By Kumar Chaudhari, GreatClipsDeal

📊 Key Findings

  • Texas is the runaway coupon capital: 221 unique coupons — 23% of every coupon we tracked, and more than the next three states combined.
  • Coupons reached 44 states and provinces in six months — including Canada.
  • Coupon volume follows franchise advertising decisions, not corporate policy or population.
  • A quiet state isn't a permanent one — regional and universal offers cover the gaps.

Ever wondered why your friend in Dallas always seems to have a haircut coupon and you never do? We counted every unique Great Clips coupon our tracker recorded from December 30, 2025 to July 6, 2026 — 960 coupons across 44 states and provinces — and mapped where they ran.

The state leaderboard

Texas alone produced 221 unique coupons — nearly one in four of everything we tracked, and more than Georgia, Arizona and Florida combined. Note the two Canadian entries: British Columbia (22) and Ontario (19) both out-couponed most US states, which is why we track Canada too.

Why the map is so uneven

Great Clips corporate doesn't run these coupons — individual franchise owners do, buying their own Facebook advertising for their own salons. So the leaderboard reflects where franchisees compete hardest for customers and spend most on ads, not where Great Clips has the most salons. Population isn't the driver either: California, with more people than Texas, produced a fifth as many coupons. And the map shifts constantly — Georgia led the board in early July after a burst of $4.99 promotions, despite Texas dominating the six-month totals.

Live in a quiet state? You have options

  • Regional and universal offers — franchisees sometimes run offers valid at any participating salon in a metro area or nationwide. These appear at the top of our homepage whenever they're live.
  • Check your state page — the state directory shows what's live for you today, updated several times a day.
  • Check back monthly — coupon activity moves. States that were silent for months (like Nevada) suddenly produced a run of offers when a local franchisee started advertising.

Methodology & honest caveats

  • Data: 960 unique coupons recorded by our tracker from Great Clips' official Facebook advertising, Dec 30, 2025 – Jul 6, 2026, attributed to the state of the salon(s) named in the offer.
  • Region-wide offers that don't name a specific salon (about 5% of coupons) are excluded from state counts.
  • Our tracker's coverage improved in June 2026, so absolute counts understate early-2026 activity; the state ranking was stable before and after the change.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which state has the most Great Clips coupons?

Texas, by a wide margin. From December 2025 through July 2026 we tracked 221 unique coupons for Texas salons — about 23% of all coupons nationwide, and more than the next three states (Georgia, Arizona, Florida) combined.

Why doesn't my state have Great Clips coupons?

Coupons are created by individual franchise owners who buy their own local advertising, so some states simply have less franchise ad activity right now. Check the regional and universal offers on our homepage — they cover salons in areas without location-specific deals — and check back monthly, because activity shifts.

Does Great Clips have coupons in Canada?

Yes. British Columbia (22 unique coupons) and Ontario (19) both appeared in our six-month tracking, out-couponing most US states. We list Canadian coupons alongside US ones.

Do more populated states get more coupons?

No — population is a poor predictor. California produced only 44 unique coupons in six months versus 221 for Texas. Franchise advertising decisions, not population or salon count, drive the coupon map.
