What 960 Real Coupons Reveal About Great Clips Prices

A six-month data study of every coupon we tracked — real prices, not promises

Published July 6, 2026 · By Kumar Chaudhari, GreatClipsDeal

📊 Key Findings

  • The most common Great Clips coupon price in 2026 is $10.99, followed closely by $9.99.
  • Sub-$6 haircut deals are real but rare: about 15% of all coupons we tracked.
  • Coupon prices have been stable all year — the typical board price stayed between $8.99 and $10.99 every month.
  • Where you live matters more than when you look: median coupon prices range from $8.99 in Texas to $15.99 in California.

If you search for Great Clips coupons, you'll find plenty of sites promising "$5.99 haircuts!" — usually with no evidence. We wanted the real answer, so we analyzed our own tracking dataset: 960 unique coupons collected from Great Clips' official Facebook advertising between December 30, 2025 and July 6, 2026, covering 44 states and provinces. Here's what Great Clips coupons actually cost in 2026.

The most common coupon price is $10.99 — not $5.99

Across 960 unique coupons, $10.99 appeared 192 times and $9.99 appeared 170 times — together making up nearly 4 in 10 of every coupon we saw. The famous ultra-cheap deals exist ($4.99, $5.00 and $5.99 offers accounted for 144 coupons, about 15%), but they're concentrated in a handful of promotional bursts, usually tied to specific salons trying to fill chairs.

The practical takeaway: if a coupon near you says $9.99 or $10.99, that's a normal, good deal — waiting around for a $5.99 offer usually means paying full price ($15–$19) in the meantime while a real $4–$5 discount expires unused. If a sub-$6 coupon does show up for your salon, treat it as a limited event and use it quickly.

Prices have been flat all year

We also checked whether coupon prices are creeping up. They aren't — at least not in the first half of 2026. The median price of the live coupon board hovered between $8.99 and $10.99 in every month from January through July, with no upward trend. Whatever is happening to regular salon prices, franchisees' coupon strategy has stayed remarkably consistent.

Your state matters more than your timing

The biggest driver of coupon price isn't when you look — it's where you live. Median coupon prices by state tell the story:

A "great deal" in California is $12.99; in Texas the same money buys the most expensive coupon on the board. This mirrors regular price differences between markets, but the gap is real either way — see our state-by-state coupon study for which states get the most offers, or check today's coupons in your state.

Methodology & honest caveats

  • Data: every coupon our automated tracker recorded from Great Clips' official Facebook advertising, Dec 30, 2025 – Jul 6, 2026 (189 consecutive days, 960 unique coupons after deduplication).
  • "Unique coupon" = one offer code. The same price at 10 salons under one code counts once.
  • A small number of offers at round prices (like $5.00) are "dollars off" promotions rather than final haircut prices; they are a minority and don't change the overall picture.
  • Our tracker's coverage improved in June 2026, so we deliberately make no claims about the number of coupons per month — only about prices, which are unaffected.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Great Clips coupon usually cost in 2026?

Based on 960 coupons we tracked from December 2025 through July 2026, the most common coupon prices are $10.99 and $9.99. The typical (median) live coupon has stayed between $8.99 and $10.99 every month of 2026.

Are $5.99 Great Clips coupons still real?

Yes, but they are the exception. Offers under $6 made up about 15% of the 960 coupons we tracked, and they usually run at specific salons for short windows. If you see one for your salon, use it before it expires.

Which state has the cheapest Great Clips coupons?

Among states with enough data in our tracking, Texas has the lowest median coupon price at $8.99, while California has the highest at $15.99. Where you live affects coupon prices more than when you look.

Are Great Clips coupon prices going up?

Not so far in 2026. The median price of live coupons stayed between $8.99 and $10.99 in every month from January through July 2026, with no upward trend.
