How to read this test

Visitors are randomly split approximately one-third per variant and keep the same assignment in this browser. Repeat popup opens and clicks do not increase the unique counts. Preview links, saved requests from before the experiment, and browsers requesting tracking protection are excluded.

Confirmed membership is verified by the WhatsApp bot. “New join observed” means the bot first saw that person outside the group, then verified membership. Someone who joins before the first check may be classified as already a member. The experiment cannot identify a visitor’s WhatsApp account until they send the request.

The enrollment period is 28 days, followed by seven days for outcomes to finish. The winner assessment uses completed conversion windows, sample-size and traffic-balance checks, and corrected comparisons across the three variants. It can legitimately end with no clear winner. The site returns to the control after enrollment ends; it does not automatically publish a winner.

These are anonymous browser-level results, not a count of distinct people across every device. Events blocked by a browser may be missing. The intervals shown are descriptive; the winner assessment separately adjusts for multiple comparisons. Individual experiment records expire after 90 days; the final aggregate report is retained.
