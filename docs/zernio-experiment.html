How to read this test

Visitors are randomly split approximately one-third per variant and keep the same assignment in this browser. Repeat popup opens and clicks do not increase the unique counts. Preview links, requests from before the experiment, and browsers requesting tracking protection are excluded.

WhatsApp messages and coupon sends are counted by our official Zernio integration. “Self-confirmed” means the customer tapped “I’ve joined”; it is not proof of group membership. “Coupon sent” means the provider accepted the send, not delivery or reading. Page exits are best-effort browser pagehide signals before clicking, not proof the visitor abandoned permanently. Closing and exit counts may overlap, and someone may return and convert later. “No click yet” includes visitors who have not clicked within the seven-day window.

The enrollment period is 28 days, followed by seven days for outcomes to finish. The winner assessment uses completed conversion windows, sample-size and traffic-balance checks, and corrected comparisons across the three variants. It can legitimately end with no clear winner. The site returns to the control after enrollment ends; it does not automatically publish a winner.

These are anonymous browser-level results, not a count of distinct people across every device. Events blocked by a browser may be missing. The intervals shown are descriptive; the winner assessment separately adjusts for multiple comparisons. Individual experiment records expire after 90 days; the final aggregate report is retained.
