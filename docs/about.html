Who Runs This Site?

GreatClipsDeal.com is built and maintained by Kumar Chaudhari, an independent data analyst — one person plus an automated tracking system, not a content farm. The site started in 2024 as a personal tool to stop missing haircut deals, and grew into the tracking dataset that now powers everything here: the live coupon board, the per-state statistics, and our data studies.

Every number published on this site is computed from our own dataset of coupons collected from Great Clips' official Facebook advertising — nothing is estimated, scraped from other coupon sites, or invented. Questions or corrections are welcome via the contact page; we fix errors when readers point them out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you affiliated with Great Clips?

No, GreatClipsDeal.com is an independent website. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Great Clips Inc.

Where do the coupons come from?

All coupons are collected from Great Clips' official Facebook advertising campaigns. These are real promotions run by Great Clips franchise owners.

How do you make money?

We may display non-intrusive advertisements and participate in affiliate programs. The coupons themselves are always free.

A coupon didn't work. What do I do?

Coupons may be location-specific or have expired. Try another coupon from our site, or contact us to report the issue.
