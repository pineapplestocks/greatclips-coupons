About Kumar

Kumar Chaudhari is an independent data analyst and the one person behind GreatClipsDeal.com. He started the site in 2024 for an unglamorous reason: he kept missing haircut deals because Great Clips coupons live in scattered, short-lived Facebook ads run by individual franchise owners — there was no single place to see them all.

So he built one. Kumar designed and maintains the automated system that scans Great Clips' official Facebook advertising several times a day, verifies each offer against the official redemption page, and organizes everything by state, city, price, and expiration date. Since December 2025 that system has tracked hundreds of unique coupons across 44 states and provinces — a dataset that now powers the live coupon board, the per-state statistics, and the site's original research.

His editorial rule is simple: every number published on this site must be computed from the tracking dataset — never estimated, never copied from another coupon site, never exaggerated to make a headline. When the data can't support a claim, the claim doesn't run.

Standards & Corrections

  • Sourcing: coupons are collected exclusively from Great Clips' official Facebook advertising and link directly to offers.greatclips.com. The full process is documented in how we verify coupons.
  • Independence: GreatClipsDeal.com is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Great Clips Inc.
  • Honest limitations: when the tracking system changes in a way that affects a statistic, the affected articles say so in their methodology notes rather than hiding it.
  • Corrections: spotted an error — a dead coupon, a wrong number, a broken page? Report it via the contact page and it gets fixed.
