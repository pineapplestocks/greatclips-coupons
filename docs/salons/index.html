How Great Clips coupons are scoped

Great Clips runs three kinds of offer: national coupons valid at participating US salons, statewide coupons, and market coupons tied to a metro area such as Chicagoland or Greater Houston. A market coupon covers every suburb in that market, not just the city named on it - which is why a Chicagoland offer works in Schaumburg or Naperville.

We map all 645 Great Clips markets to the cities inside them, so each city page shows the offers that genuinely reach it. Participation is ultimately set by each franchise owner, so confirm at the salon.
